Graded, not guessed

Every match tells you how sure we are.

A merge in HubSpot cannot be undone, so a tool that hands you one undifferentiated list is handing you the risk. Every group is graded by how much evidence supports it.

ConfirmedTwo signals

The name and email local part match, and something independent agrees — related domains, a shared company, or the same phone number. Strong enough to act on without reading every pair.

LikelyOne signal

A hard identifier matches — last name and phone, or full name and company — but nothing corroborates the identity. Usually right. Worth a human on each pair.

Name onlyContext

Same full name, nothing else agreeing. On a large database these are mostly different people who share a name. Shown so you know we looked, not so you act on them.

We'd rather show you a smaller number. Grading like this means the headline figure is lower than a tool that counts every name collision as a duplicate. That's the point — the number you see is the number you can trust.

Fair questions

Things worth asking first.

HubSpot already has a duplicate tool. Why this?

HubSpot's tool matches on the email address, which catches the easy cases — the same address entered twice. It cannot catch the same human at a new employer, which is the version that quietly grows for years. Against our own fixture of 50 confirmed pairs, email matching found none of them.

Will it merge my contacts?

No, and it can't. Hublytix requests read-only access, so it has no ability to change anything in your HubSpot account. You merge in HubSpot's own tool, using the list we give you. A merge is permanent, and we'd rather that decision — and the audit trail — stayed where your team already works.

What do you store?

Record IDs and the groups they belong to. Names, emails, job titles and companies are fetched live from HubSpot each time you look and are never written to our database. Your HubSpot tokens are encrypted at rest.

Do I need admin rights to install it?

No. Installing an app that asks for write access requires a HubSpot Super Admin. Ours is read-only, so any user with access to the portal can approve it.

How big a portal can it handle?

It reads every contact, not a sample. A 137,000-contact portal completes in a little over two minutes. Larger portals take proportionally longer and you can close the tab — the scan keeps running.

What does the export actually contain?

One row per contact in a duplicate group, with its HubSpot Record ID, name, email, job title and company, the group it belongs to, its confidence tier, which record we'd keep, and a blank column for your decision. The columns are named so you can map them straight into a HubSpot import.

Is Full Export a subscription?

No. Full Export is $9.42 one-time and includes 15 scans. Free stays at 3 scans per HubSpot account. No monthly plan.
