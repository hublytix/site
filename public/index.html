FAQ

Questions founders ask

Why is the call only ₹249?

It's a founding price for the first 20 founders while I build case studies. After that the call is ₹999 for 30 minutes. Either way, the fee is credited toward the AI Growth Audit if you book it within 14 days.

What happens on the call?

Before the call you fill in a short form: store URL, monthly revenue band, ad spend, team size, your top two problems and the tools you use. On the call we go through where AI can save money or time first. Within 24 hours you get a 1-page AI plan with three ranked quick wins you can act on with or without me.

Who is this for?

Founder-led Indian D2C brands doing roughly ₹1–20 Cr a year, usually on Shopify, spending on Meta ads, with a team of 3–25 and no in-house AI person. Beauty and personal care, fashion, food and beverage, and health and supplements are the best fit.

Who is this not for?

Brands under about ₹50 lakh a year, which usually don't have the budget yet, and brands above ₹150 Cr, which typically have in-house teams and large agencies.

Do I need to buy any AI tools?

Not to start. The audit is vendor-neutral and I take no tool commissions. Where a tool makes sense, like a WhatsApp platform for support and COD confirmation, I'll recommend it and set it up on your account.

Will you push me into a retainer?

No. The call ends with an offer of the audit if it makes sense, with no hard pitch. Many brands only need the audit or one sprint. The retainer exists for brands that want the work run every month.

How do I pay and book?

Bookings and payments run through Topmate, which takes UPI. Until the booking page goes live, the button opens an email to hello@hublytix.ai and I'll reply with a slot.

What about the Hublytix HubSpot tool?

It's still running. The HubSpot duplicate-contact finder has moved to its own page, and existing accounts still sign in at app.hublytix.ai.
