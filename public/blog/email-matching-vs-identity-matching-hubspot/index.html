Email matching vs identity matching in HubSpot

HubSpot auto-dedupes on email at create time. Identity matching finds the same person across emails — before you merge.

HubSpot’s automatic dedupe keys off email. Same address → same contact. New address → new contact. That’s email matching.

Identity matching asks a different question: is this the same person, even when the email changed? That’s the gap behind most “we cleaned duplicates and they came back” stories — and the gap our HubSpot duplicate contacts pillar covers at a high level. This post is the how.

Who this is for: Ops / RevOps hitting contact limits after job changes or enrichment imports. Not for: a Manage Duplicates click-path tutorial.

Why HubSpot still creates duplicates when the email is new

At create time, HubSpot is trying to avoid two records with the same email — not to prove two different emails are one human. So a job change, a second inbox, or an enrichment list with a fresh address often lands as a brand-new contact on your billable count.

Manage Duplicates (Pro+) can later suggest pairs using name, email, phone, and more. Useful — and a different job from blocking the create. It’s paid-tier, plan-capped on how many pairs you see, and contacts/companies only. Ops Hub’s ML layer is the nearest native identity-ish tool; it still doesn’t replace “don’t create the second contact when the email is new.”

What identity matching actually checks

Before you merge, you want evidence — not a name collision:

  • Confirmed — strong signals agree (e.g. name + local-part + independent corroboration)
  • Likely — a hard identifier matches; worth a human look
  • Name only — usually different people; don’t merge to “save” a contact seat

A merge in HubSpot can’t be undone. Identity matching is how you decide what’s safe — not how you auto-rewrite the CRM.
